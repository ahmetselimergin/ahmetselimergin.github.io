Trial Guard Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

In short

Trial Guard has no accounts and never connects to your bank. The trials and subscriptions you track stay on your phone. The only data leaving your device is what Google AdMob uses to show ads in the free version, and what Google Play handles when you buy Pro.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is saved locally, on your phone only: service names, prices, currencies, billing cycles, renewal and trial end dates, reminder settings, categories and notes. Reminders are scheduled by Android on your device. We (the developer) never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Trial Guard has no sign-in, does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or microphone, does not read your bank or card data, and does not include any analytics service of its own.

Screenshots you scan

If you choose "Fill in from a screenshot", you pick one image with Android's photo picker. Its text is read on your device by Google ML Kit text recognition, which works offline. The image and its text are not uploaded, stored by the app, or shared. Only the price and date you choose to save are kept.

Backups

Pro users can export their list to a JSON file. The file is saved wherever you choose (for example your phone's storage or your own Google Drive) and is never sent to us. Importing reads only the file you pick.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad at the bottom of the main screen through Google AdMob. Pro removes all ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language),
  • ad interactions (views, clicks) and diagnostic data about ad delivery.

Your tracked subscriptions are never shared with AdMob. Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and explains its use of data in partner apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Trial Guard Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro is owned and remembers the answer on your device so Pro keeps working offline. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Payments privacy notice.

Your choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), Trial Guard asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Notifications: you can turn reminders off per item, or for the whole app in Android settings.

Children

Trial Guard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because the developer does not store personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best handled through your consent settings above and Google's tools at myaccount.google.com. You can always contact us for help.

Security and changes

Data sent by the AdMob SDK and Google Play is encrypted in transit. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
