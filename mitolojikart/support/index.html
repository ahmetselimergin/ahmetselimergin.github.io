Tamga — Support

Tamga: Legends of the Steppe is a card duel game based on Turkic mythology.

Contact us

Found a bug, having trouble or have an idea? Write to us with the link below: describe the problem in the title, and add your device and game version to the description.

Open a support request

Requests are public on GitHub; please do not include personal information.

FAQ

Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Do I need internet? No. When online, the game downloads the latest card data.

Are there ads or purchases? No.

How do I see the rules again? The question mark button on the main menu explains the rules.
